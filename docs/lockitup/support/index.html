Help with Lock It Up — common questions about scoring, sync, and your data.

Frequently asked

Which devices can run Lock It Up?

Version 1 supports iPhone on iOS 26 or later and Android phones on Android 8.0 or later. The initial release uses portrait phone layouts.

Do I need a Widget Corner account?

No. Lock It Up does not create a Widget Corner account and has no subscription, advertising, or marketing login. iCloud or Google Play Games authentication is used only by the platform’s optional completed-game sync.

What syncs between devices?

Only completed-game history that has been confirmed by choosing Rematch or New Game is eligible to sync. Rules, active games, paused games, and a newly completed result that has not yet been confirmed stay on the device. iPhones sync only with other iPhones through the user’s private CloudKit database; Android devices sync only with Android devices through Google Play Games.

Sync is not working.

Scoring still works without sync. On iPhone, confirm both devices use the same Apple Account, can access iCloud, and have a network connection, then reopen Lock It Up. On Android, open Stats and use the sync control to sign in to Google Play Games or retry after reconnecting.

How do I correct a scoring mistake?

During an active game, use Undo to clear the current bids or remove the last scored round, then enter it again. Lock It Up recalculates the score from the remaining rounds.

Can I export or delete completed stats on iPhone?

Yes. Open Stats, then choose Export completed games for a shareable JSON file. Choose Delete completed stats to remove completed history from that iPhone and the user’s private CloudKit database. Active and paused games are preserved.

How do I permanently remove Android history?

First close Lock It Up. Clear its app storage or uninstall it on every Android device that still has local Lock It Up history, and do not reopen it. Then delete Lock It Up’s per-game data from your Google Play Games profile. Clearing storage removes local rules and games in progress too. If a device retains local completed history, opening it can recreate the cloud save.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email support@widgetcorner.com. Include your device model and OS version if possible.
